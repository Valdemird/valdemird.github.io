A **wall jump** is a jump from a wall instead of the floor: in the air, against a wall, you press jump and get thrown up and away at 400 px/s. It comes with a **wall slide**, which caps the fall at 130 px/s while you push toward the wall, and that's the catch: the input that keeps you on the wall is the same one that ruins the jump off it.

<h2 id="five-ways-it-fails">Wall jump assists: five fixes the player never sees</h2>

Raw physics breaks the wall jump in five small places. Each assist fixes one, and T switches all five off. [Mega Man X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mega_Man_X_%28video_game%29) (1993) already let you slide down or jump off nearly any wall.

| Without help | The assist | In the lab |
|---|---|---|
| You must touch the wall to the pixel | Detection margin | 5 px (1.5 px after T) |
| You left the wall just before pressing | Wall coyote time | 100 ms |
| Holding toward the wall cancels the push | Control lock | stick ignored for 140 ms |
| Letting go of the stick stalls you halfway | Carried momentum | air drag ×0.1 until you touch the stick |
| A short tap leaves you lower than you started | Minimum height | 220 ms before the jump can be cut |

<h2 id="how-to-implement">How to implement a wall jump</h2>

1. **Detect the wall with a margin.** Shift the player's collision shape a few pixels to each side and check for overlap. Every tick that finds a wall reloads the wall coyote timer.
2. **Set the jump velocity outright**, away from the wall at 85% of a normal jump's height, then start the lock, the carry and the minimum height.
3. **Expect the stick to stay on the wall.** Without the lock, air control beats the push and sends you back to the same wall.

```gdscript
if wall_dir != 0:
	_wall_coyote = params.wall_coyote_time if Lab.on(&"wall_assist") else 0.0
# ...
# WALL SLIDE: pegado a una pared y empujando hacia ella, la caída se frena.
var sliding := not on_floor and wall_dir != 0 and input_x == wall_dir and velocity.y > 0.0
if sliding:
	velocity.y = minf(velocity.y, p.wall_slide_speed)
# ...

func _do_wall_jump(margin: bool = false) -> void:
	var from_dir := wall_dir if wall_dir != 0 else _last_wall_dir
	velocity.x = -from_dir * params.wall_jump_speed_x
	velocity.y = -params.jump_velocity * sqrt(params.wall_jump_height_ratio)
	# ...
	# BLOQUEO DE CONTROL: durante unos ms se ignora el stick. Sin esto, quien
	# mantiene pulsado "hacia la pared" anula el impulso y vuelve a ella al instante.
	_wall_jump_lock = params.wall_jump_lock_time if Lab.on(&"wall_assist") else 0.0
	_wall_jump_hold = params.wall_jump_min_time if Lab.on(&"wall_assist") else 0.0
	# INERCIA: hasta que se toque el stick, el aire apenas frena este salto.
	_wall_carry = Lab.on(&"wall_assist")
```
</CodeBlock>

While `_wall_jump_lock` runs, the horizontal movement returns before it reads the stick.

<h2 id="quick-answers">Quick answers</h2>

<h3 id="wall-coyote-time">How long should wall coyote time be?</h3>

100 ms, the same as [coyote time on ledges](/blog/coyote-time-jump-buffer/). [Celeste](https://github.com/NoelFB/Celeste/blob/master/Source/Player/Player.cs) has no wall timer and gives a wider reach instead: 3 px on an 8 px body.

<h3 id="wall-slide-speed">How fast should a wall slide be?</h3>

Slow enough to aim: 130 px/s here, about a seventh of the 900 px/s free fall.

<h3 id="single-wall-climbing">Should players be able to climb a single wall?</h3>

Decide it on purpose. Here each wall gives one jump, then stops gripping until you touch the floor, bounce or jump from another wall. Celeste allows unlimited wall jumps and makes climbing cost stamina.
