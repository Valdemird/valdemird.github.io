A **pogo attack** is a down-slash in the air that bounces you up when it connects, spikes included. The bounce is a fixed **600 px/s**, about 2.2 tiles, and it recharges your dash and air jump.

| | Value | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Bounce | 600 px/s, about 2.2 tiles | the same height every time |
| Down-slash startup | 0 ms | pressing just before contact still bounces |
| Armed time | 240 ms | pressing early, at the top of the jump, still bounces |
| Late grace on spikes | 40 ms | pressing just after touching still bounces, if you hold down |

<h2 id="what-is-a-pogo-attack">Pogo attack: hits that work as a floor</h2>

Enemies and spikes stop being things to avoid and become, for an instant, something to stand on. Hollow Knight is the usual reference: its [wiki's combat page](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Combat) says a down attack in the air lets you "pogo off an enemy, often multiple times in a row." The bounce ignores [variable jump height](/blog/variable-jump-height/), so it's always the same 2.2 tiles and a level can be measured in bounces. Spikes carry a hurtbox with no health that blocks every hit, 34 px tall against 10 px of lethal zone: the slash connects, nothing takes damage, and you bounce.

<h2 id="down-slash-timing">Down-slash timing: why it can't copy the forward slash</h2>

The forward slash has 35 ms of startup and 100 ms of [active time](/blog/attack-startup-active-recovery/), right for hitting something in front of you. When you're falling at 500 to 700 px/s, that leaves a few hundredths of a second to press. So the down-slash comes out with no startup, stays armed for 240 ms, and spikes wait 40 ms for a player who lands already holding down. Someone who lands without holding down wasn't trying to bounce.

<h2 id="how-to-implement">How to implement a pogo attack</h2>

1. **Pick the direction when the swing starts.** Down if the player holds down in the air.
2. **When a down-slash connects, call the same bounce a spring uses:** a fixed upward speed, with the dash and air jumps refilled in that one place.
3. **Give the down-slash its own timing:** no startup, a long armed time, a short grace on spikes.
4. **Make the spikes' bounce zone taller than the lethal one,** or the spikes win ties.

<h2 id="quick-answers">Quick answers</h2>

<h3 id="pogo-refill-dash">Should a pogo refill the dash?</h3>

Yes, if your game has one. Here it refills the dash, air jumps and wall jumps, like landing, so a row of targets can replace a floor.

<h3 id="pogo-height">How high should a pogo bounce be?</h3>

Lower than a full jump, and always the same: 600 px/s, about 2.2 tiles against a 3.6-tile jump. Releasing the button doesn't cut it.
