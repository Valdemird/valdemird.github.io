<h2 id="what-is-telegraphing">Telegraphing: a pose, a ring and a sound</h2>

The warning is a state of its own with a fixed length, and the attack only comes out when it ends. The body leans back and shakes, a ring closes over its head to tell you *when*, and a rising chirp covers the enemy you aren't looking at. Because the length never changes, after two or three tries your hands know it. Press T and the charger is exactly as fast, but it stops telling you what it's about to do.

<h2 id="how-to-implement">How to implement a telegraphed attack</h2>

1. Make the warning its own state, with a fixed length per attack. The attack only comes out when that timer runs out.
2. Drive every cue from that one timer: the ring closes with the fraction of the warning that has passed, and the sound plays the moment the state starts.
3. Pick the attack's direction when the warning starts and never correct it. An attack that steers after the tell punishes the player for reading it.
4. Give the enemy poise during the warning. If a hit could interrupt it, mashing would cancel every attack.

<CodeBlock filename="addons/combat_kit/enemies/enemy.gd">
```gdscript
## TELEGRAFIAR: entra en el estado de aviso. El ataque sale cuando `windup_done()`.
func telegraph(attack: StringName, seconds: float) -> void:
	attack_name = attack
	set_state(&"windup")
	windup_total = seconds if Lab.on(&"telegraph") else minf(seconds, 0.05)
	Combat.enemy_telegraphed.emit(self, attack, windup_total)


func windup_done() -> bool:
	return state == &"windup" and state_time >= windup_total
```
</CodeBlock>

<CodeBlock filename="addons/combat_kit/enemies/charger.gd">
```gdscript
func _think(delta: float) -> void:
	match state:
		&"patrol":
			patrol(walk_speed, delta)
			if is_on_floor() and sees_target():
				face_target()
				telegraph(&"embestida", windup_time)
		&"windup":
			if windup_done():
				contact.attack = &"embestida"
				release(&"charge")
```
</CodeBlock>

`face_target()` runs once, before `telegraph()`, so the charge goes exactly where the warning pointed.

<h2 id="quick-answers">Quick answers</h2>

<h3 id="how-long-telegraph">How long should an attack telegraph be?</h3>

Regular enemies here warn for 0.45 s (the hopper) to 0.65 s (the drone). The shortest real warning is the boss's hammer in phase two, 0.425 s. At 0.05 s it stops being a warning.

<h3 id="telegraph-too-easy">Does telegraphing make a game too easy?</h3>

No. The warning gives you the information, and you still have to act on it in time. To make a fight harder, shorten the warning or chain attacks: the boss cuts every warning by 15% in its second phase.

<h3 id="telegraph-sound">Should a telegraph have a sound?</h3>

Yes. The pose and the ring only work if you're looking at the enemy. A chirp the moment the warning starts makes an attack from the edge of the screen readable too.
