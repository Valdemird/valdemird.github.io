A **risk-reward heal** costs you twice: you earn the energy by landing hits, then spend **0.9 s** standing still to use it, and any hit cancels it. It turns healing from a panic button into a question you ask mid-fight: do I have a second right now?

<h2 id="what-is-risk-reward-healing">Risk-reward healing: pay with hits, then with time</h2>

Every hit that connects gives 11 energy, and a whiff or a hit blocked by a shield gives nothing. One mask costs 33, so three hits buy a heal: then you hold V for 0.9 s, on the ground and unable to move. The loop regulates difficulty on its own, because whoever plays well gets more heals.

Hollow Knight's Focus runs on almost the same numbers: a meter of 99, 11 SOUL per hit and 33 per mask ([SOUL](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Soul) and [Focus](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Focus) on the community wiki).

<h2 id="how-to-implement">How to implement a risk-reward heal</h2>

1. Add energy where a hit is resolved, and only when it connects: the same moment as the [hit confirm](/blog/hit-confirm-hitstop/).
2. Make the heal a timer that only runs while the button is held and the player is on the floor, with movement switched off.
3. Spend the energy when the timer completes, not when it starts. You only pay for a heal you actually got.
4. After a hit, require letting go and pressing again. Otherwise a held button restarts the heal as soon as the knockback ends, with the enemy still on top of you.

		add_energy(-params.heal_cost)
		var amount := health.heal(params.heal_amount)

	_stop_heal(true)
	_heal_blocked = true

`add_energy(-params.heal_cost)` only runs once the timer completes, so a heal cut short costs time, not energy.

<h2 id="quick-answers">Quick answers</h2>

<h3 id="make-healing-risky">How do you make healing risky in a game?</h3>

Make it take time, take away movement while it runs and let any hit cancel it. Here that's 0.9 s on the ground with no control.

<h3 id="heal-duration">How long should a heal take?</h3>

Around a second. This one takes 0.9 s per mask; Hollow Knight's Focus takes about 1.14 s for the first and 0.89 s for each after. Much shorter and there is no risk; much longer and nobody tries it outside an empty room.

<h3 id="cancelled-heal-cost">Should an interrupted heal still cost resources?</h3>

Pick one on purpose. Hollow Knight wastes the SOUL consumed, while here the energy is only spent when the heal completes. Charging at the end makes players try more often.
