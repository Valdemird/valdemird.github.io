A **dodge dash** grants invincibility frames while it lasts, so you go through attacks instead of over them. Here that's the **0.16 s** of the dash plus 0.05 s for the exit, and a **0.45 s** cooldown keeps mashing from making you untouchable.

<h2 id="what-is-a-dodge-dash">Dash i-frames: through the attack, not over it</h2>

Jumping dodges what comes along the floor, but it leaves you in the air and far from the enemy. A sideways dash dodges and places you: you come out on the other side, behind whoever attacked, with their recovery still ahead of them. The extra 0.05 s covers the instant after the dash when you may still overlap what you went through. Hollow Knight's [Shade Cloak](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Shade+Cloak) works the same way: it lets you "dash through enemies and their attacks without taking damage."

<h2 id="dash-cooldown-rule">The cooldown rule: at least twice the protection</h2>

If the cooldown counts from the start of the dash, someone mashing the button is protected at most protection ÷ cooldown of the time. With 0.21 s of protection, a 0.22 s cooldown would make you untouchable 95% of the time, and the dash would become the fastest way to walk. At 0.45 s it's 47% at most, and between two dashes there's always a vulnerable gap of 0.24 s, longer than the protected one. The Shade Cloak recharges in about 1.5 s.

<h2 id="how-to-implement">How to implement dash i-frames</h2>

1. **Don't add a dodge state.** When the dash starts, grant invulnerability for its length plus a small margin, on the same timer the i-frames after a hit use.
2. **Cancel any attack in progress when the dash starts.** Otherwise a slash thrown just before travels inside the dash, untouchable and hitting at once.
3. **Set the cooldown to at least twice the protection,** counted from the start of the dash.
4. **Don't protect against hazards.** Spikes and pits ignore invulnerability, or a dodge would cross any spike pit 3.6 tiles at a time.

```gdscript
## Lo que dura de más la protección del dash respecto al dash: cubre la salida.
const DASH_GUARD_EXTRA := 0.05
# ...
	player.dash_started.connect(_on_dash_started)
# ...
func _on_dash_started(_direction: Vector2) -> void:
	_cancel_attack()
	if Lab.on(&"dash_iframes"):
		health.grant_invulnerability(player.params.dash_time + DASH_GUARD_EXTRA)
```

`grant_invulnerability()` fills the same timer as the [i-frames after a hit](/blog/health-damage-invincibility-frames/) and keeps the larger value, so a dash never shortens protection you already had.

<h2 id="quick-answers">Quick answers</h2>

<h3 id="how-long-dash-iframes">How long should dash i-frames last?</h3>

The length of the dash plus a small margin for the exit: 0.16 s plus 0.05 s, so 0.21 s here.

<h3 id="dash-spikes">Does a dodge dash protect you from spikes?</h3>

Not here, and Hollow Knight's Shade Cloak doesn't either. The dodge is for attacks, things that come at you with a warning.
